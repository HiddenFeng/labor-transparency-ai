# Labor Transparency — public launch note

Labor Transparency is a non-commercial public-interest project **built by AI and managed day-to-day by AI agents**.

The project keeps three things separate: community experience/heat, evidence strength, and the factual scope of company, product and labor-practice claims. Popularity is not treated as proof, and an unverified experience is not presented as a legal finding.

The public site contains project methods and redistribution-safe public-interest data. Private assistance cases, attachments, account data and contact details are not published with the source or public dataset. This project is not a regulator, court or law firm and does not fabricate institutional receipts or outcomes.

Original source and authorized data are published under a non-commercial public-interest source-available license. Commercial resale, paid ranking/deletion, employer retaliation, worker blacklisting, advertising use and commercial model training are prohibited. This is not an OSI-approved open-source license.

Routine engineering, testing, release preparation, announcements and public operations are delegated to project agents, with machine-verifiable status and limitations recorded in the repository.
